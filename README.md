# Smart Student Planner – UX Group Project

A student-focused planning and productivity system designed to help university students manage time, commuting stress, and focus. This project showcases the full UX process, including research-driven design, wireframes, and a high-fidelity interactive prototype created in **Figma**.
(Please check out links at the bottom)
---

##  Project Overview

This group project was completed as part of **ITEC 3230 (Designing User Interfaces)** and focuses on improving the daily experience of university students by addressing common challenges such as:

- Poor time management
- Unpredictable commute delays
- Difficulty maintaining focus and motivation
- Lack of structured study routines

The solution, **Smart Student Planner**, provides smart scheduling, focus sessions, and productivity tracking in a single, user-friendly interface.

---

##  Problem Statement

University students often struggle to balance classes, commuting, studying, and personal well-being. Existing tools either lack flexibility or fail to account for real-world factors like transit delays and burnout.

**Our goal** was to design a system that:
- Centralizes schedules and deadlines
- Adapts plans based on commute conditions
- Encourages focused study through short, gamified sessions
- Supports healthier productivity habits

---

##  User Personas

### Persona 1 – Time-Conscious Commuter
- Commutes long distances to campus
- Needs reliable daily planning
- Wants to use travel and downtime productively

### Persona 2 – Wellness-Focused On-Campus Student
- Studies in short bursts
- Struggles with burnout and motivation
- Benefits from gamified focus sessions and reminders

---

##  Key Use Cases

### 1. Plan My Day
- View daily schedule and time gaps
- Adjust plans based on commute conditions
- Receive a structured daily plan with study blocks and breaks

### 2. Start Focus Session
- Choose a short focus duration (e.g., 25–30 minutes)
- Enter distraction-free focus mode
- Earn badges and track productivity progress

---

##  UX Design Process

The project followed the **Double Diamond design process**:

### Discover & Define
- User research and problem identification
- Creation of user profiles and personas
- Definition of functional and non-functional requirements

### Design & Develop
- Low-fidelity sketches and ideation
- Structured wireframes
- High-fidelity interactive mockups in Figma
